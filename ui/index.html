        Help · workflow, roles and limitations
        
A record-based maintenance tool, not flight clearance

        

          Operators record self-reported usage and findings. Technicians record
          swaps, inspections, completed work and retirement. A different
          reviewer verifies completed work only after a fresh passing inspection
          and checks current part conditions. Administrators provision
          aircraft/components and publish versioned standards. The OS owner
          provisions staff accounts through the local CLI.
        

        

          Blocked means a known configured limit/condition failed. Unknown means
          missing or contradictory evidence. Review means a warning band or
          watch item. Within recorded limits means only that the configured
          checks passed using supplied records—not that the drone is safe,
          lawful or cleared to fly. Manufacturer requirements, preflight
          inspection and responsible human decisions remain necessary.
        

        

          Usage is attributed to installed parts at recording time; operating
          cycles are not battery equivalent full charge cycles. Missing
          component/history/inspection data is never guessed. Assessments retain
          their original inputs; any domain change, server-clock rollback or
          inspection deadline can make them stale. Independent acknowledgement
          does not remove bad findings.
        

        

          The app is one local staff workspace, not multi-tenant cloud hosting.
          All staff can read organizational maintenance notes and JSON evidence.
          Downloads are private records; share only through authorized channels.
          The OS owner can access disk files. No cloud analytics, device
          control, real maintenance measurements, manufacturer endorsement or
          compliance certification is included. Source documentation has setup,
          schemas, troubleshooting and measured test evidence.
