# Research and engineering interpretation

Reviewed 2026-10-22 UTC. References guide engineering choices; none approves the sample limits, app or a flight. No copied manufacturer data is bundled.

- [FAA UAS policy library](https://www.faa.gov/uas/resources/policy_library) links applicable regulatory and advisory material, including AC 107-2A. [Official AC 107-2A metadata](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/advisory_circulars/index.cfm/go/document.information/documentID/1038977) describes Small UAS Part 107 guidance. Engineering interpretation: trace maintenance evidence and keep responsible human/manufacturer/preflight decisions separate from a favourable software badge. This is not a comprehensive jurisdiction-specific legal analysis, and the app does not compute legal flight eligibility.
- [SQLite transaction documentation](https://sqlite.org/lang_transaction.html) describes single-writer transactions and coherent reader snapshots. Engineering choice: `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` for atomic part relocation, attribution and generation sealing; failed writes close/roll back, readers see a coherent domain state. No unbounded concurrent-write throughput claim.
- [OWASP Logging Cheat Sheet](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Logging_Cheat_Sheet.html) discusses appropriate event capture and confidentiality/integrity risks. Engineering choice: intentional private actor/evidence ledger, omit credentials, bounded notes/capacities, authenticated exports and honest hash-chain limitations. Hashes alone are not trusted signatures or proof of physical work.

The implementation's strict contracts, self-only UI assets, same-origin/CSRF checks, opaque local sessions and reviewed lockfiles complement this evidence model. Direct attribution tests, exact threshold oracles and fresh independent repair verification are project-specific engineering decisions, not claims mandated or certified by these sources. API and browser tests verify implemented behavior; domain experts must independently choose actual maintenance standards and operational procedures.
